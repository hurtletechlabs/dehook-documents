Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Dehook app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Adithya D (collectively, the "Application"). Adithya D is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect your personal information on any server, and does not send it to the Service Provider. Everything the Application uses is processed and stored only on your device, in the Application's private storage. This includes:

  • your first name and your answers during setup (optional);
  • the apps you choose to block, your limits, scheduled blocks and settings;
  • how long you spend in each app (screen time), kept as one daily total for up to 35 days;
  • the list of apps installed on your device, used only so you can choose which apps to block;
  • journal entries you write, focus sessions, streaks and daily app-open counts;
  • for messages held back during a block, the sender's name and time only (never the message text), kept until the block ends.


Permissions and Sensitive Data

The Application uses the following permissions only for the purposes described, on your device. Nothing obtained through them is stored beyond what is listed above, or sent off your device.

  • Accessibility Service: to detect when you open an app you chose to block, to recognise blocked feeds (such as Reels, Shorts or Explore) by reading the screen layout of those apps, to show block screens, and to move you out of a blocked feed. The Application does not read or record your messages, posts, passwords or anything you type.
  • Notification access (optional, only if you turn on notification filtering): to decide whether a notification from a social app is a message from a person or something else (such as a like or a shared reel), to hide the rest, and to hold messages during a block. Notification content is never stored or sent.
  • Usage access: to show your screen time and Hook score.
  • Display over other apps: to show block screens and the session timer.
  • Battery optimisation exemption and closing background apps: to keep blocking working and to close a blocked app when its time is up.

You can turn off any of these permissions at any time in your device settings.


Cookies and tracking technologies

The Application does not use cookies, advertising, analytics or other tracking technologies.
